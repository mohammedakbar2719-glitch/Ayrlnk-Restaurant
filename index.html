About Us

The story behind AYRLNK

🍽️
Est. 2019

We Cook With Passion

AYRLNK was born out of a simple dream — to bring the richest culinary experiences to Hyderabad. Founded in 2019 by Chef Aryan and Lynk Enterprises, we set out to bridge the gap between traditional Indian flavors and contemporary global cuisine.

Every dish on our menu is a result of months of research, experimentation, and a deep respect for the ingredients we use. From our slow-cooked biryanis to our wood-fired pizzas, we pour our hearts into every plate.

Today, AYRLNK serves over 500 happy customers daily and has become a landmark dining destination in the city.
